What am I looking at?

Try a small experiment.

  1. Choose Sudden price shock. Which average reacts faster?
  2. Switch to Volatility squeeze. Watch the bands contract, then expand.
  3. Reduce the replay slider. Do the indicators change before a new price appears?

These indicators describe price history. A band touch, crossover, or threshold reading is not a guaranteed prediction or an order instruction.
