About SurfingCoder

Follow the question.
Show the work.

SurfingCoder is a place for software projects, data analysis, AI experiments, surfing insights, and whatever else rewards a closer look.

The connection is curiosity: a result that seems odd, a rule that changes the picture, or a small tool that makes a complicated question easier to answer.

What you can expect

Analysis with a trail back to the evidence. When an article depends on numbers, it should explain where they came from, how they were calculated, and what the calculation can—and cannot—establish.

Software with a practical purpose. Projects start with a question or a problem. The useful part is what the code lets someone understand or do.

Room to revise. A dated snapshot is a dated snapshot. Sources, assumptions and material corrections belong alongside the conclusion.
