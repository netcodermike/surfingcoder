How to ride

Carve the swell and hurl boards at diving mantas, squid, clawed crabs, and sharks. Dodge creatures and their stinging bubbles. Three wipeouts end the ride.

One surfer. Steer with the arrows or A and D.

Bonus sets

Every third set is Shell Run or Glass Barrel. Hit passing shells with your boards. Catch all 21 for 2,100 extra points, with no chance of a wipeout during the bonus.
