WELCOME TO THE BREAK

Ride the reef.

Steer your surfer through the channels and collect every pearl to discover a new break. You have three boards—colliding with sea life costs one.

Big turquoise swells give you a few seconds to chase sea creatures for bonus points. Grab the pineapple before it drifts away, and use the side channel to wrap around the lagoon.

Steer: arrows, WASD, swipe, or touch buttons.
Pause: P or Escape. Sound: M.
