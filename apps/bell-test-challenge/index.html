Rules and model

The referee gives each player a random bit. Players win by giving equal answers unless both questions are 1, when their answers must differ. Always giving equal answers achieves the best classical win probability of 75%. A short sample can finish above or below that probability.

Each quantum round starts with a new simulated Bell pair. In the real-plane measurement bases, the probability of equal answers is cos²(A − B). CHSH S combines four correlations, with an ideal quantum win ceiling of 85.4%.
