About the model

The simulation evolves a normalized Gaussian packet with the one-dimensional Schrödinger equation using a Crank–Nicolson solver. Axes use scaled units with ℏ = m = 1. The plot shows a 56-unit window. Region probabilities integrate over the full grid.
