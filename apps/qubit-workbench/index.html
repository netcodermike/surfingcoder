How the probabilities work

A pure qubit is a pair of complex amplitudes, α|0⟩ + β|1⟩, with |α|² + |β|² = 1. The Bloch vector is (2 Re(α*β), 2 Im(α*β), |α|² − |β|²), where α* is the complex conjugate of α.

The chance of the positive outcome in the selected basis is (1 + r)/2, where r is that basis’s Bloch coordinate. Each shot independently samples these probabilities. Changing the state or basis starts a fresh tally.
